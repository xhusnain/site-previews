What you'll find

  • New vinyl and CDs

    Our main floor carries new vinyl records and new and used CDs in every genre, from rock, jazz and metal to hip-hop, country, punk and classical.

  • Used records, tapes and movies

    The second floor is loaded with used vinyl, cassettes, DVDs, VHS tapes, music and movie posters, books and magazines.

  • Buy, sell, trade

    Bring in your records, CDs and movies to sell or trade.

  • Special orders

    New stock arrives every week, and we're happy to look into ordering titles we don't have on hand.

  • Turntables and accessories

    We carry Audio-Technica turntables and accessories.

  • Local music and tickets

    CDs and vinyl by Windsor artists, in-store performances, tickets for shows at local venues, and gift cards in any amount.

About Dr. Disc

Established in 1982, Dr. Disc is Windsor's longest-running independent music store. We're avid promoters of Windsor's music scene and look forward to filling your musical prescription.
