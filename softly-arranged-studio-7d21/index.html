How it works

From inquiry to celebration

Whether it is a bouquet, a flower bar or a whole event, every order starts the same way: with your colors, your occasion and your date.

  1. 01

    Inquire

    Tell us what we can arrange: flowers, an experience or event planning, with your date, occasion and vision.

  2. 02

    Consult

    We'll contact you to talk through colors, occasion and your preferred date, and to discuss design, availability and pricing.

  3. 03

    Design

    Every order is custom. Your flowers or your event are planned around your culture, your style and your priorities.

  4. 04

    Set up on the day

    For events, Teesha and her team bring it together with trusted vendor connections, so you can be a guest at your own celebration.

  5. 05

    Celebrate

    Enjoy the day. At a flower bar, every guest leaves with a bouquet they built themselves, wrapped to take home.
