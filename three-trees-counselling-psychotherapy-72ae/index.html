What I help with

  • Trauma and post-traumatic growth

    Support moving from interpersonal, nature-based, systemic and intergenerational trauma toward a fulfilling life.

  • Grief and loss

    Space to work through grief, loss and the questions that come with it.

  • Life transitions

    Stress, anxiety, sadness, guilt and shame, and questions of identity and life purpose.

  • Brainspotting and body-based therapy

    Brainspotting, somatic and nature-based approaches that reach parts of the brain talk therapy alone can't.

  • Expressive arts

    Contemplative photography, nature, sandtray work and music alongside talk therapy.

  • Online and in person

    Virtual sessions Monday to Friday, and in-person sessions at the Trauma & Grief Institute in Bells Corners.

About Catherine

Catherine is a Registered Psychotherapist with the College of Registered Psychotherapists of Ontario (CRPO #10913). She completed a Master's degree in counselling psychology at Yorkville University, specializing in trauma and post-traumatic growth. Compassion, creativity, warmth, humour, integrity and research guide her practice. The standard fee is $170 for a 50-minute session, with a sliding scale for some clients. She is currently accepting clients by referral.
