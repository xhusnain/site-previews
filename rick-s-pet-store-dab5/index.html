About Rick's Pet Stores

Rick's Pet Stores are full-line pet stores with large inventories, and one of the largest suppliers to aquarium hobbyists in eastern Ontario. Visit us in Petawawa at 20 Wolfe Avenue, or at our Pembroke, Renfrew and Arnprior locations.
