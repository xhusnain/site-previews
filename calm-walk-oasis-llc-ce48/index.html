What we offer

  • 8-week accelerated group

    Structured educational group sessions online over Zoom, one hour each. Groups are continuous, so there is never a waiting list.

  • Court-ordered and self-referred

    For people involved in custody, assault, property damage and similar cases, and for anyone who wants to manage anger better.

  • Paperwork for your case

    Letters of enrollment, monthly progress reports for referral sources, and a certificate of completion.

  • What the sessions cover

    The meaning and power of anger, abusive and controlling behavior, communication, conflict resolution, assertion vs aggression, emotional intelligence, stress and forgiveness.

About Calm Walk Oasis

Calm Walk Oasis is an anger and aggression management program affiliated with The Anger Management Institute in Oak Brook, Illinois, approved and endorsed by the National Anger Management Association (NAMA), and a partner of the Prince George's County Diversion Program. All staff are NAMA members, Certified Anger Management Specialists (CAMS-I) and Certified Domestic Violence Specialists (CDVS-I). Calm Walk Oasis is a Service Disabled Veteran Owned Small Business and a Prince George's County certified Minority Business Enterprise and County Based Small Business.
