Programs and counseling

  • Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP)

    2.1 level of care and 1.0 outpatient program for substance use disorder, with case management and peer support.

  • DUI classes and evaluations

    ACT Program DUI classes certified by Prime for Life, MIP services and chemical dependency evaluations.

  • Mental health and co-occurring disorders

    Mental health counseling, addiction counseling and dual diagnosis treatment.

  • Anger management and domestic violence

    Anger management and domestic violence counseling, plus a batterer intervention program using the Duluth Model.

  • Sex and porn addiction

    Counseling for sex and porn addiction, and sex offender assessment and treatment.

  • Spiritual and pastoral counseling

    Spiritual and pastoral counseling, the Twelve Steps, and meditation and relaxation sessions.

About us

CMP Power of Healing provides counseling and coping skills to people who are ready to make changes in their lives. Counselor Carlos Malaverparada is a LAC, LADAC, NAMA CAMS-II and LCPC-C. We are in-network with insurers, including Blue Cross Blue Shield of Montana, and accept private pay. Sessions run 45 to 60 minutes and are available online or face-to-face.
