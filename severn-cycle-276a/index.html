Run by a Red Seal technician

Severn Cycle Service is owned by Scott Smith, a Red Seal Trade Certified Motorcycle Technician with over 20 years of formal training and experience in motorcycle repair and fabrication. The shop is fully licensed and works on all makes and models, with safety as the top priority. The team: Scott and Jeremy, both motorcycle technicians. We support the Georgian Bay Thunder Run, Community Reach Orillia and the Orillia Channel Cats Swim Club.
