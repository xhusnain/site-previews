Dr. Joseph Pohorly's winery

Joseph's Estate Wines is a family-owned and family-run winery in Niagara-on-the-Lake. Dr. Joseph Pohorly was one of Niagara's first winery owners, founding Newark Winery in 1979, and in 1983 he made the first Icewine for Hillebrand. Today, with associate winemaker Katherine Reid, the winery makes a full range of VQA varietals, Vidal Icewine, fruit wines, sherry and port in a fruit-forward style true to Dr. Joseph.
