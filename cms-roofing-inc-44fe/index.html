Our history

Christian Miller opened C. Miller and Sons on Broadway in Fort Wayne in 1913. His sons kept it going through the Depression, and in 1980 Stan Miller started CMS Roofing with his son Bradley and son-in-law Fred to focus on commercial roofing. Today the company is run by the 4th and 5th generations. Accomplishments include Carlisle's 500 Hall of Fame and Perfection awards and GAF Master Select status.
