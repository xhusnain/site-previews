Doula support

Steady, practical support so you walk into birth and the weeks after knowing what you want.

“Every pregnant mom deserves to feel prepared and supported.”Wasidah

  1. Prenatal prep

    Time together before the birth to talk through what's coming, answer your questions and get you and your partner ready.

  2. Birth preferences

    We put your wishes into a clear birth preferences plan you can share with your care team, so your voice is in the room.

  3. Fourth-trimester planning

    A plan for the weeks after the baby arrives: rest, recovery, help at home and who to call when you need it.

Birth preferences and planning for the fourth trimester are topics I share about often. Ask me anything about them in your inquiry.
