About Broken Wheel

Broken Wheel Campground sits in Petersburg, NY, where New York, Vermont and Massachusetts meet. Come for a change of routine and some rest, or use us as a base for hiking, biking, history and culture around Albany, Bennington and the Berkshires. Register at the caboose when you arrive. It also has candy, souvenirs, gifts and camping guides.
