What we're known for

  • Scotty's classics

    The two-patty Hot Hamburger and BBQ Back Ribs.

  • House-made pasta

    Sauces made in house. Chicken Alfredo and Baked Shrimp Linguine are regulars' favourites.

  • Sandwiches, salmon and salads

    Beef dip sandwiches, salmon dishes and hearty meal-sized salads. And if you like spice, try the Jambalaya.

  • Groups and occasions

    Private booths and moveable tables for large groups, whether it's a table for two or a celebration.

  • The bar

    Big-screen TVs, draught on tap and a wine list. Watch the game or wait for your table.

A local Barrie restaurant

Scotty's opened on Minet's Point Road on May 1st, 1994 and is now on the corner of Yonge Street and Big Bay Point Road, behind The Beer Store. The menu is traditional with a modern flare, and while the true Scotty's experience is dining in, everything is available for take-out too.
