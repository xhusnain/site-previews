What's at Small's

  • The front bar

    An art deco-era bar stocked with more than 50 beers, from local and national craft labels to European brands and cold 24 oz. cans.

  • The live room

    National and local bands and DJs on the calendar, down the hall from the bar.

  • Tickets

    Advance tickets are paperless and held at will call under your name. Transferable, not refundable.

  • Booking

    Bands and promoters can get in touch about playing Small's.

About Small's

Small's was established in 1999 in a building that was originally a bank, dating back to 1923. It's been featured in Maxim, Travel and Leisure and Guitar World, and our 'Keep it a Small's World After All' compilation on Bandcamp collects 49 bands who've played here over the past 20 years.
