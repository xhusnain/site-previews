At Highbanks

  • RV sites and SuperSites

    Nightly, weekly and monthly stays in wooded RV sites, plus larger SuperSites.

  • Cabins

    Cabins with a one-week minimum. Bring your own linens.

  • Pontoon boat rentals

    Five pontoon boats for 6 to 8 people, half day or whole day, open seven days a week. Call 386-668-2350.

  • 72-slip marina

    Boat slips, a gas dock, boat launch, fish cleaning station, non-ethanol fuel and LP gas.

  • Country Store

    Food, supplies, live bait and tackle, gifts, hats and t-shirts.

  • Campground amenities

    Swimming pool, 3,000 sq ft clubhouse, pool room, two laundries, a catch-and-release pond and horseshoe pits.

Camelot on the River

Highbanks Marina & Campresort is known as the "Camelot on the River": 25 wooded acres on the St. Johns River with a full marina, country store and the Swamp House restaurant next door, with live music and a 150-seat full bar (reservations 386-668-8891).
