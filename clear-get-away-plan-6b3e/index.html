One thing to fix first

Your site tells Google that its real address is cleangetaway.uk, with an "n". That domain doesn't exist. What it can cost you: Google may not index cleargetaway.uk properly, and the address it picks up for your business is wrong. Fixing it takes a few minutes, and I'm happy to do it for free.

How a waste job usually reaches you

Every extra step someone has to take is a chance for them to ring the next company on Google instead.

  1. They search"rubbish removal Daventry", "garage clearance Rugby", "WEEE collection Northampton". Showing up here is SEO, and town-by-town pages help a lot.
  2. They check you outReviews, photos of real jobs, your licence and insurance. This is where trust is won or lost.
  3. They ask for a priceToday that means sending photos by email or phone and waiting. The faster the answer, the more jobs you win.
  4. They bookA date, a time window and a confirmation by text. Same-day slots are your edge, so the site should show them.
  5. After the jobWaste transfer note emailed automatically, then a request for a Google review. Reviews feed straight back into step 1.
