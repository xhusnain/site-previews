About Breau Disposal

We're a family owned and operated business on the beautiful Miramichi, providing complete disposal service from curbside pickup to commercial and industrial waste management. We believe the key to protecting our environment is clean, up-to-date equipment and recycling, and we'll advise you on handling, recycling and disposing of most types of discarded materials. No customer is too large or too small.
