About Awareness

Awareness Massage Therapy Clinic was established in 1995 in the heart of downtown Regina. Our therapists are registered and in good standing with the Massage Therapist Association of Saskatchewan and give each client a personalized treatment plan after an assessment. The clinic has a team of therapists and extended hours of practice.
