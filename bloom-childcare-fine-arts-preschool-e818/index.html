About Bloom

At Bloom, children learn new skills and express their creativity in a safe, nurturing environment. Our programs combine arts and crafts, music and movement, and group-based learning, led by certified, caring staff who build strong connections with every child. Our licensed facility is a former elementary school with large classrooms, a gymnasium, computer lab, music room and library, set beside a green belt with three outdoor playgrounds.
