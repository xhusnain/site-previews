The office work that runs itself

The jobs in your administrator advert that don't need a person: these happen automatically, so the office handles the conversations that do.

Day-before visit texts

When a round is planned, every customer on it gets a text with the day. Rain delay? One tap moves the whole round and re-texts everyone.

Hi Sarah, your spring treatment from Apex Lawns is booked for Thursday. Please leave the side gate open. Reply R to rearrange.

Missed visits logged from the van

Greenkeepers tap "missed" with a reason (gate locked, dog out, waterlogged). The customer is told, and the visit goes back on the next round.

We couldn't get to your back lawn today (gate locked). We've moved you to Tuesday, no need to do anything.

Payments and reviews

Friendly payment reminders go out on day 3 and day 10. After each visit, happy customers get a link to leave a Google review.
