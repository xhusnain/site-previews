About Cadmans

Cadmans Montreal Bagels is Canadian owned and operated, founded in Ottawa by Ranjeev Sharma, who has 20+ years of experience in the bagel business. His philosophy is simple: provide the best products and even better customer service. Come visit our open-concept kitchen and watch the bagels being made.
