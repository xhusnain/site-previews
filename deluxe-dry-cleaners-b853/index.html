About Deluxe

The Jamieson family has owned and operated Deluxe Dry Cleaners for over 30 years. In 2009 we acquired the rights to GreenEarth Cleaning, and in 2014 we installed a second GreenEarth machine to keep up with demand. We received the Environmental Excellence in Business Award from Dalhousie University's Eco-Efficiency Centre in 2011, and owner Martin Jamieson was named 2013 Industry Person of the Year by Fabricare Magazine.
