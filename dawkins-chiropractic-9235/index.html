About the clinic

Dawkins Chiropractic Clinic, formerly the Kevin Matheson Chiropractic Clinic, has been part of Sarnia's healthcare community for over 30 years. Dr. Wes Dawkins took over in 2010. Born and raised in Sarnia, he earned his BSc at the University of Guelph and his Doctor of Chiropractic at the Canadian Memorial Chiropractic College, with training in orthotics, Active Release Techniques and medical acupuncture at McMaster University. The clinic is a fully accessible, restored two-storey century home on Wellington Street, just west of the Five Corners.
