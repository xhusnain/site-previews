About Nova 3

Nova 3 Engineering is a Manitoba corporation founded in 1981 by Victor Thielmann, P.Eng. and the late Mike Mitousis. John Guenther, P.Eng. joined as head of mechanical in 2001, and Dan Zilinski, P.Eng. joined the ownership group in 2024. Our team of professional engineers, senior design technologists, CAD operators and contract administrators has worked on health care, institutional, education, residential, commercial and industrial projects in provinces and territories across Canada.
