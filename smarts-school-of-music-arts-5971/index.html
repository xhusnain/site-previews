What we offer

  • Lessons on 20+ instruments

    Piano, keyboard, voice, acoustic, electric, classical and bass guitar, ukulele, drums, violin, viola, cello, flute, clarinet, oboe, saxophone, trumpet, French horn, trombone, baritone and tuba.

  • Qualified teachers

    Every teacher has post-secondary training or is a professional musician, and lessons focus on the songs and styles you want to learn.

  • Soundproofed, secure studios

    Studios built to block over 90% of sound, each with an HD camera feeding live to the waiting area so parents can watch.

  • Instrument rentals

    Rent most instruments you'll need, and put 100% of your rental fees toward buying one.

  • Recitals and exam prep

    Two free recitals a year. No pressure to do exams, but we can prepare you for RCM exams or university auditions if that's your goal.

  • Student portal and online lessons

    Check your schedule, lesson notes and invoices, get automatic reminders, or take lessons online.

A message from the owner

I grew up in a musical family and went to an arts school where music was part of every day. As an adult I learned how much music helps with memory, language and math skills, and emotional wellbeing, and how often it takes a back seat to other activities. That's why we started School of Music Arts: to give everyone in our community, young and old, the chance to learn and enjoy music. You're never too old to learn.
