About Hidden Springs

Hidden Springs is a family owned and oriented campground with activities for kids and adults. We're 4 miles from Rocky Gap Casino Resort, home to Maryland's only Jack Nicklaus Signature golf course, plus kayaking, swimming and fishing. Or just sit back and enjoy the simple life. Note: most GPS units take you the wrong way. Take I-68 to exit 50 only, then Pleasant Valley Rd., which becomes Beans Cove Rd. at the Pa. line. We're on the left.
