Your cottage

  • 2-bedroom cottages

    Sleep 4 comfortably, up to 6 (extra charge for 2 more). Winterized, with electric heat on separate thermostats and a 3 piece bathroom.

  • Fully equipped

    Fridge, stove, dishes and cooking utensils. Each cottage has a 3 season sunroom, wood burning fireplace, fire pit, picnic table and its own boat dock.

  • On the trails

    We're on top ATV and snowmobile trails, close to cross-country ski trails. Stay with us and ride from your doorstep.

  • On the lake

    Rent a canoe or aluminum boat, or bring your own and launch from ours. Swim off your dock or relax on the beach.

  • Rates

    From Cdn $200/night and from Cdn $1400/week plus taxes, 2 night minimum.

About Kushog Korners

Kushog Korners sits directly on Highway 35 between Highways 117 and 118 at Pine Springs Road, about 13 km from Muskoka. Our self-catering cottages feel just like home. Relax in front of the fireplace after a day of sledding, jump off your dock after a day of ATV-ing, or just stretch out under the shade trees with a good book. Wi-Fi is available throughout. Bring your sheets, pillow slips and towels.
