What we do

  • Rack and barrel plating

    Zinc finishes from commercial thickness of 0.0001 in. (2.5 um) up to 0.0008 in. (20 um) specification deposits for extended corrosion protection.

  • Post-plating treatments

    Surface treatments for extended corrosion resistance, colour identification and enhanced lubricity.

  • Stress relief baking

    Baking in ovens with open perforated baskets to eliminate hydrogen embrittlement in hardened steels.

  • Certified finishes

    Finishes certified on request to automotive, military, nuclear, electrical, plumbing, appliance, commercial and ASTM specifications.

  • Pick-up and delivery

    Our trucking fleet picks up and delivers, with less than 48-hour turnaround on orders.

Family-founded in 1955

Joseph Z. Holody founded Holody Electro-Plating in Guelph in 1955 after 10 years in the industry. Today we run a 40,000 sq. ft. plating facility with an on-site lab for continuous testing and our own waste treatment. Our philosophy is simple: quality, service and price, with every order back on time.
