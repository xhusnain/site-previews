About A Plus International

A Plus International Corporation is a Canadian company dedicated to import and wholesale, in response to the growing demand for sunglasses, underwear and pantyhose. Our founders saw a gap in the market for high quality products at affordable prices, and combined their knowledge of Asian manufacturing with their marketing skills to build a range for all tastes and all seasons.
