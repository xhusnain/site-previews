Family owned since 1959

We're a family business, owned and operated in St. Clair County since 1959. Our family has helped customers grow and care for their soil and plants for over 65 years. Our retail store grew out of the original lime and fertilizer business and has sold feed, lawn and garden supplies and pumpkins for over 30 years. Find us on Wadhams Road north of Ravenswood and the railroad tracks.
