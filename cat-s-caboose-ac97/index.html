What we offer

  • Restaurant and bar

    Our full menu, known for wings and burgers, with 20 taps of beer. Open seven days a week.

  • Live entertainment

    A stage and a proper audio system for live bands.

  • Parties and events

    Corporate gatherings, holiday and office parties, birthdays, retirements, christenings, stag and does and graduations. Up to 120 in the dining room and 100 in the bar.

  • Games room

    Great for kids' birthday parties and team parties.

  • Catering and takeout

    Catering for small to large functions, plus a large takeout menu. A la carte, buffet or prix fixe.

A Glenridge institution

Located in the heart of old Glenridge, Cat's Caboose is one of St. Catharines' oldest and most established restaurants: the place for live entertainment and great food, with free parking and a trained staff that has been recognized by local readers' choice voting.
