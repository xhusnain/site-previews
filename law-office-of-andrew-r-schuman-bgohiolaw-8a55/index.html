About Attorney Schuman

Born and raised in Staten Island, Andrew Schuman went to law school at the University of New Hampshire School of Law (then Franklin Pierce Law Center) and started out handling felony and juvenile cases as an assistant prosecutor for Wood and Ashtabula Counties. After several years on the civil side, he opened his own general practice in 2009. He's also a licensed private investigator, and when you call, you speak directly to an attorney.
