About the farm

We are the Kirbys: Marie, Robin, Maggie and Aerron and the kids, with help from CSA working-share members and volunteers. We grow nearly three acres of organic vegetables, many of them unusual varieties, and raise chickens, ducks, turkeys, sheep, cattle and two draft horses on pasture. We're slowly turning the farm toward agroforestry, planting fruit trees, berry bushes and native trees throughout.
