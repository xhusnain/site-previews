What we work on

  • Anger and emotional regulation

    For physicians facing complaints, professionalism concerns or reactions that keep getting them into trouble under pressure.

  • Conflict and communication

    Friction with colleagues, staff or administration, and conversations that keep going badly.

  • Leadership and career transitions

    New roles, changing responsibilities, and questions about retirement.

  • Relationships and couples work

    When the pressures of medicine follow you home and strain a marriage or family life.

  • Meaning and burnout

    For doctors who have lost the thread of why they do this work.

  • Referrals from organizations

    Hospitals, medical groups and credentialing bodies can refer a talented, disruptive or at-risk physician, with progress reported under an agreed framework.

About Dr. Hudson

Patrick Hudson, MD is a retired board-certified plastic and hand surgeon who spent more than three decades in clinical practice and now coaches physicians and surgeons exclusively. He is a psychotherapist and board-certified coach with advanced degrees in Counseling, Medical Ethics and Liberal Arts, a Fellow of the American College of Surgeons and a Fellow of the National Anger Management Association. Coaching is individual, confidential and available nationwide by Zoom, sometimes using assessments such as DiSC. Documentation is available for physicians who self-claim AMA PRA Category 2 Credit.
