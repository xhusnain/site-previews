FAQ

Questions before you book?

Do I need to provide supplies?

No. We bring all supplies and equipment. If you prefer eco-friendly products or have something you want us to use, just tell us.

Do I need to be home?

Not at all. Many clients leave a key or door code. We confirm access details before every first visit.

How do recurring discounts work?

Weekly, bi-weekly and monthly cleans cost less per visit than a one-time clean. The saving shows in your estimate.

Do you clean offices and commercial spaces?

Yes. We clean offices, clinics, studios and retail spaces, usually after hours. Book a free walkthrough for a fixed quote.

Which areas do you serve?

All of Ottawa, including Kanata, Orléans, Barrhaven, Nepean, Gloucester, Westboro, the Glebe and Centretown.
