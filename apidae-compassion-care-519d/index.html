How we help at home

  • Personal care

    Help with bathing, grooming, dressing and other personal care, done with dignity.

  • Respite care

    A short period of rest for family members and other unpaid caregivers.

  • Companionship and patient advocacy

    One-on-one companionship and support, including while a loved one is in hospital.

  • Transportation

    Rides to and from activities, errands and appointments.

  • Medication reminders and fall prevention

    Reminders to take prescribed medications, plus fall prevention exercises.

  • Light housekeeping and meal prep

    Tidying, removing tripping hazards, and preparing ingredients and meals.

About Apidae

Apidae is the Latin name for the bee family: thousands of bees working together to care for their community. We bring that same spirit to caring for the people who built our communities. We work with families to build a care plan that fills gaps in service so seniors feel safe, supported and engaged at home or in their care facility.
