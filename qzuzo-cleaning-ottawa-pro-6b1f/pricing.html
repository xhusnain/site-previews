    FAQ

    Good questions.

    Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

    No. We bring all supplies and equipment. If you prefer eco-friendly products or have something you would like us to use, just let us know.

    Do I need to be home during the clean?

    Not at all. Many clients leave a key or entry code. We will confirm access details with you before the visit.

    How do recurring discounts work?

    Weekly, bi-weekly and monthly cleans are priced lower than a one-time visit. The discount is shown in your estimate.

    Can I reschedule or cancel?

    Yes. Contact us at least 24 hours before your booking and we will move it to a time that works.

    Which areas do you cover?

    Ottawa and surrounding neighbourhoods, including Kanata, Orléans, Barrhaven, Nepean and Gloucester.
