A storefront for local makers

Benjamin's Bodega is owned by Sage Homes. If you're talking to us online, you're speaking with Brittney, mom of four, whose kids' names inspired the storefront. Brittney grew up in Dundas and moved to Guelph with her partner Kyle, a carpenter and general contractor, in 2017. The Bodega started as a way to make things easier for local makers, giving them a storefront instead of hauling everything to markets and pop-ups.
