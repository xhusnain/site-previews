What to do after a crash

The minutes and days after a wreck are stressful. A few simple steps can help protect your health and your claim.

General information only, not legal advice. Every situation is different.

  1. Get safe and call 911Move out of traffic if you can, check on everyone involved and make sure police are called.
  2. Get checked by a doctorSome injuries don't show up right away. Getting medical care also creates a record of what happened to you.
  3. Document the scenePhotos of the vehicles, the road, your injuries, and the truck's markings or DOT number. Get names and contacts of witnesses.
  4. Be careful with insurersYou don't have to give a recorded statement to the other side's insurance company before you understand your rights.
  5. Talk to a lawyerA free case review can help you understand your options before deadlines pass.
