Classes and counseling

  • Anger management classes

    6, 8, 12, 26 and 52 week programs plus accelerated options, in group or individual sessions. Group classes meet online Tuesday evenings and Saturday afternoons.

  • Domestic violence classes

    Batterers' intervention classes for men and women in single-gender groups, approved by the Los Angeles County Department of Probation. Rolling admission.

  • Parenting classes

    A 10-week Triple P (Positive Parenting Program) curriculum for parents of children 0-12 and teens 12-17, in group or individual format.

  • Individual counseling

    Help with stress, anxiety, anger, depression and relationship issues, building self-awareness and practical tools.

  • Family counseling

    A family systems approach to break stressful patterns and build healthier communication at home.

  • Divorce mediation

    Mediation support for couples working through separation and family conflict.

About NVAMC

Co-founded by Karen Fincher, MA, LMFT, and Brad Klimovitch, MA, CAMS-II, North Valley Anger Management Consultants meets the requirements of the Los Angeles County Superior Court, Family Court and Department of Probation. Classes are taught by certified facilitators trained at the California Cognitive Behavioral Institute and run evenings and weekends over a secure, HIPAA compliant video platform.
