The story

your Pavlova linocut here

It started with a wedding gift. I made treats for a friend's wedding and got so many compliments that I kept going.

The Little Hearth launched just over a year ago, and it's a family affair. My sister designed the branding, including a linocut of my cat Pavlova that became our logo. She also carved the stamp I press onto every label by hand.

The flavors come from places I've lived and traveled, and from the things I love to eat and drink.
