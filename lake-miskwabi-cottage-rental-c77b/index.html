The cottage

  • Sleeps 10

    5 bedrooms: 3 Queen, 1 Queen + twin and 1 Double. Groups of friends or families welcome, adults generally over 25.

  • Hot tub and firepit

    A 7+ seat hot tub with lake views, available all seasons, on a granite terrace with firepit.

  • On the water

    200 feet of southeast-facing private frontage, shore deck and dock with a ladder to deep water. Canoe and 2 kayaks provided.

  • Inside

    Open plan kitchen and dining, upstairs lounge with fireplace and satellite TV, winterised sunroom, fast wifi, A/C and dedicated work spaces.

  • What's included

    Wifi, electricity, EV charge point, firewood, a tank of propane for the barbecue and a well-stocked kitchen. Heating and snowploughing in winter.

  • Dogs welcome

    One or two well-behaved dogs are welcome. Please let us know.

About the cottage

Our 4-season cottage sits on 2.37 private, well-treed acres on clean, clear Lake Miskwabi, 20 minutes from the village of Haliburton. Next door is a natural conservation area with 800 feet of undeveloped shoreline, and you'll see deer, loons, beavers, turtles and wild turkeys. July and August are weekly rentals, Friday to Friday; dates are flexible the rest of the year. Licensed by Dysart et al, Short Term Rental License STR-25-00002. Hosted by Dianne & Andrew Stewart.
