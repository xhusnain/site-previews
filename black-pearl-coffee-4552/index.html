About Black Pearl

Black Pearl was established in Winnipeg in 2000 and roasts premium, certified Fair Trade and certified organic coffees from around the world. Our roasting facility is fronted by a retail store, and our staff's involvement in roasting and developing coffee makes them a knowledgeable part of your visit. You'll also find our coffees at Food Fare, Red River Co-op and other Winnipeg stores. Orders over 4 pounds need two business days' notice and prepayment.
