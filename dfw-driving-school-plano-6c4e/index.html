About us

DFW Driving School helps teens and adults across Plano and Frisco learn to drive safely, from the classroom course to behind-the-wheel hours and the DPS road test. Patient instructors, clear steps and two local offices, with enrollment and payment online.

FAQ

Before you enroll

Do you teach adults as well as teens?

Yes. There is a 6-hour adult permit class, adult driving lessons and in-car enrollment.

Can you help with the DPS road test?

Yes. We offer road test prep sessions and DPS road tests.

Can I take a course online?

Online driving courses and defensive driving are available, and we can help with Drive Time account setup.

Which location should I choose?

Whichever is closer to home or school. Classes and lessons run at both Plano and Frisco.
