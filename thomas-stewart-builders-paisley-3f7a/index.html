FAQ

Before you get in touch

Do you work for homeowners as well as businesses?

Yes. We look after both residential and commercial clients across West Central Scotland.

Are the tradespeople your own staff?

Our core trades are employed directly, so the people who quote the work are the people who stand behind it.

Can you handle several trades on one job?

That is the point of the company: roofing, joinery, plumbing, electrical, plastering and decorating are coordinated through one office.

What are your office hours?

Monday to Thursday 8:00 to 16:30, Friday 8:00 to 15:30. Emergency call-outs are available 24/7.
