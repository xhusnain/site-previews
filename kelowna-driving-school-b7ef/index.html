About us

Kelowna Driving School is a small, home-based company approved, certified and licensed under the Motor Vehicle Act. Owner Ravi Baidwan has 40+ years of professional driving experience in cabs, buses and semi trucks across British Columbia, and currently drives transit buses in Kelowna. Our manager and second instructor, Balraj Deol, also works for Kelowna Transit.
