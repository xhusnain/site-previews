Courses we teach

  • Standard and Emergency First Aid

    CSA Intermediate and Basic Level C first aid with CPR/AED, plus recertification courses (Canadian Red Cross).

  • Healthcare provider training

    BLS, BLS renewal, ACLS and PALS through the Heart and Stroke Foundation, plus oxygen therapy and airway management.

  • Advanced, remote and wilderness first aid

    Advanced First Aid + BLS, Remote First Aid and 40-hour Wilderness First Aid.

  • Youth courses

    Babysitting, Stay Safe! (Home Alone) and Standard Child Care Level B first aid.

  • H2S Alive and online courses

    H2S Alive with Energy Safety Canada, OH&S online courses and online naloxone training.

  • Group bookings

    Workplace-approved onsite training for groups of five or more across Southern Alberta, or private groups at our Cochrane classroom.

About CETS

CETS is a safety training company based in Cochrane. Owner Alex was born and raised here, is a Primary Care Paramedic and has taught First Aid and CPR since 2017. Chelsea, our administrator and instructor, is a Registered Nurse with over 15 years of experience, including a decade in pediatric and neonatal ICU at Alberta Children's Hospital. We aim to make training hands-on, current and enjoyable.
