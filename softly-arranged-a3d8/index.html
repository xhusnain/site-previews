The flower bar

A table of fresh stems your guests build into their own bouquet. It gives people something to do, something to talk about, and something to take home.

  1. Guests pick their stems from the buckets on the bar.
  2. I help them put the bunch together.
  3. It's wrapped in kraft paper and ribbon, ready to take home.
