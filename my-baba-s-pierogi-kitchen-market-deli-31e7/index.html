Meet Baba

My Baba's Kitchen is a family-owned and operated business. The recipes at our Ukrainian-style deli come from my grandmother (Baba) and my father. We've been eating good food our whole lives, and now we want to share it with you. You'll also find us at the Innisfil Farmers Market on Thursdays 1-6pm and the Bradford Farmers Market on Saturdays 9am-1pm.
