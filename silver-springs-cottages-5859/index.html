Stay at Silver Springs

  • Nine cottages

    Five 1-bedroom, three 2-bedroom and one 3-bedroom cottage, room for 30 people in total. Tastefully furnished with well-equipped kitchens. Wheelchair access available.

  • Families and groups

    Ideal for family holidays, romantic getaways, family reunions, corporate retreats and workshops.

  • Fishing and hunting

    Elephant Lake is one of the best Muskie lakes in Ontario, with bass, pike, trout and walleye. The Highlands have excellent deer, moose and bear populations.

  • Beach and boats

    A safe, sandy beach for swimming. Bring your own boat or rent one of ours.

  • Trails all year

    21 acres of forest, 5 km from Algonquin Park's trails, and minutes from groomed ski, ATV and snowmobile trails (just off TOP Trail E).

About Silver Springs

Silver Springs Cottage Resort sits on Elephant Lake, a shallow, fertile lake with over 22 miles of shoreline. As a housekeeping resort, families can cook their own meals on their own schedule, and kids can make friends with other families staying at the resort. There's something going on in the Highlands every season, from the Haliburton Highland Games and Art in the Park to fall studio tours, Maplefest and the Haliburton Dogsled Races.
