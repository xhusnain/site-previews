Stay with us

  • Seasonal sites

    Over 100 serviced seasonal sites with full hook-ups, 30 amp individually metered service, water and sewer. Open May 10th to October long weekend Monday.

  • Cottage rentals

    3-bedroom cottages for one family unit, up to 6 people, with fridge, stove, barbecue, fire pit and picnic table. Weekly and weekend rentals.

  • Boat docks and storage

    Dock rentals for boats 19' or less, pontoon and Seadoo slips by the day, weekend, week or season, plus boat and trailer storage and a launch.

  • Heated pool and beach

    Swim at the beach or in the heated pool, open early June until the first Tuesday after Labour Day.

  • Activities

    Volleyball, horseshoes, soccer and a playground, plus Social Committee events: long weekend dances, a kids' fishing tournament, bingo, Christmas in July and Halloween.

About Friendly Acres

Friendly Acres Park sits on the Trent-Severn Waterway, with the village of Hastings, the 'Ultimate Fishing Town', just to the west and Campbellford minutes to the east. The park was redeveloped in 2017 with new seasonal sites and secure access, and it's now under new, family ownership. Walleye, bass, pike, muskellunge, perch and crappie are all in season through the year. Groceries, a bank, pharmacy and gas are 8 km away in Hastings.
