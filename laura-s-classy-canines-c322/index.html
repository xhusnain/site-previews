What we offer

  • Grooming

    Full grooms, bath and brush packages and puppy packages for any breed. We groom one dog at a time, so no dog waits in a cage. Walk-in nail trims, ear cleaning and teeth brushing too.

  • Doggy daycare

    Cage-free daycare with two supervised play areas, small groups by size and temperament, rubber flooring and a fenced yard. Hourly, half day, full day and extended day.

  • Training with KB

    Positive reinforcement, fear-free training with KB Singh: puppy basics to advanced obedience, recall, and help with fear and anxiety. Classes and private lessons.

  • Retail

    Food, treats, toys, grooming supplies, dental care, supplements and stylish attire, with many Canadian-made products.

About Laura

I'm Laura Decker, owner of Laura's Classy Canines. I was born and raised in Riverside, graduated as a Registered Veterinary Technician from St. Clair College in 2004 and completed a certified dog grooming course in Ottawa. I opened my first salon in 2008 and I'm a proud member of the Ontario Dog Groomers Association. My team researches the latest in daycare health and safety, nutrition and grooming products so your dog gets the best care in a safe, cage-free space.
