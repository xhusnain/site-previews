Counseling services

  • Anger management

    A 12-module program covering anger awareness, calming techniques, conflict resolution, stress and mindfulness, with experience working with narcissistic behavior.

  • Marriage counseling and education

    "Divorce-Proof Your Marriage" classes on communication, expectations, managing emotions, fighting fairly and financial decisions, plus counseling for couples.

  • Temperament counseling

    Creation Therapy, a temperament-based approach from the National Christian Counselors Association, starting with a temperament analysis.

  • Grief and loss

    Support through the loss of a loved one or pet, a marriage, a job, health or finances.

  • Individual counseling

    Help with depression, stress, guilt, relationships, pre-marital questions, career, dependency and ex-offender counseling.

  • Prison ministry

    Counseling and ministry for people who are incarcerated.

About Dr. Butcher

Rev. Dr. Earl V. Butcher is a Licensed Clinical Pastoral Counselor, Licensed Temperament Therapist and Certified Anger Management Specialist II with the National Anger Management Association. He is Professor of Pastoral Counseling at Agape Seminary & College in Louisville, KY, founder and director of Trinity Christian Counseling Academy in West Virginia, and a Board Certified Mental Health Coach. Special programs are available for clients facing financial difficulty.
