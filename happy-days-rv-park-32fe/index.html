What we offer

  • Large RV sites

    30' x 60' sites with 50 amp and 30 amp service and full hookups.

  • Seasonal and monthly stays

    Daily, weekly, monthly and yearly rates, plus the 7/5 Stay & Store plan. Group rates for caravans, rallies and clubs.

  • Homes for sale or rent

    Park models and fifth wheels for sale or rent right in the park.

  • Activities

    Heated pool, rec hall, billiards, shuffleboard, bocce, horseshoes, a craft room, poker room and bingo.

  • Pet friendly

    Pets are welcome, with a dog park and dog walk.

  • On-site services

    Laundry, bath and showers, Wi-Fi, RV storage and a dump station.

Why Happy Days

Happy Days is open all year and close to Busch Gardens, the Florida Aquarium, Gulf beaches, Tampa Bay sports, Moffitt Cancer Center and the Orlando theme parks. From I-75 exit 279, head east on SR-54 about 10 miles to Allen Road; turn right and we're less than half a mile on your right.
