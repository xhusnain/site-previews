About Runar

I've been on the other side of the couch

After years of training, I felt first-hand what tightness, ongoing aches and unresolved injuries do to the body. That's why I moved into sports massage and breathwork: to help people move better, recover faster, and not reach that point.

I work with office professionals, active people and athletes. Sessions combine sports massage with breathwork to support both the muscles and the nervous system.

Questions

Before you book

Where are sessions held?

At Waterloo Bodystation, a few minutes' walk from Waterloo station.

Do I need to do anything before a session?

Wear something comfortable and tell me about any injuries when you arrive.

Can I cancel or move my booking?

Yes, you can move or cancel online up to 24 hours before.

Is breathwork safe for everyone?

Most people, yes. If you're pregnant or have a heart or breathing condition, mention it and the session is adapted.
