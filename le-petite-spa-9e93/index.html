About Le Petite Spa

Le Petite Spa believes that reducing stress and tension is key to the balance of mind, body and soul. Our treatments are designed for the whole body in a quiet, tranquil setting and are provided by our trained staff. Deposits are required on group bookings, and we ask for 48 hours' notice to cancel.
