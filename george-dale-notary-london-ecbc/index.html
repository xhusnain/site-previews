How I can help

  • Notarizing and certified true copies

    Official witnessing of your signature and certified true copies of original documents. Red or gold seals available on request.

  • Commissioner of oaths

    Affidavits and statutory declarations, including common law status, OSAP, child subsidy and custodianship declarations.

  • Travel consent letters

    Parental consent to travel documents, witnessed and signed so you're ready to go.

  • Virtual signing

    Upload your documents and ID when you book, then sign online over Zoom, FaceTime or WhatsApp using SignNow. The signed document is sent straight back to you.

  • Wills and powers of attorney

    A simple, affordable way to get your will and powers of attorney in place. Wills from $500 for an individual and $700 for a couple; powers of attorney from $75 per document.

  • House calls

    House calls are available within the City of London for an additional fee.

About George Dale

George Dale is a practising lawyer, notary public and commissioner of oaths in London, Ontario. Appointments are available on weekdays at 135 Albert St., and on evenings and weekends curbside at 1284 Eagletrace Drive. Please bring valid government-issued photo ID to your appointment.
