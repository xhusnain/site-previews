About Thorpe Construction

Thorpe Construction was founded by Jason Thorpe and has been in the commercial concrete business since 1996. Incorporated in 2002 and based in Brandon, the company employs 40 to 50 concrete professionals specializing in commercial and industrial flatwork and formwork, and serves all regions of Manitoba and Saskatchewan.
