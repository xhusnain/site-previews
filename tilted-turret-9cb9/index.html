What's on

  • Pinball machines

    New and classic machines, from Stern's Godzilla, Jaws and Jurassic Park to Elvira's House of Horrors and Cactus Canyon. Every machine is kept 100% playable.

  • Pizzas and panzos

    Panzerotti, tavern style, traditional and Chicago style. Build your own or pick a signature like The Classic, Meat Lovers or Nacho.

  • Smash burgers

    Locally sourced beef with fresh-cut fries: the Royale, the Double Royale and the Double Jackpot.

  • Comfort classics

    Wraps, chicken tenders, turkey club, hot turkey sandwich and fish and chips with in-house roasted turkey and Atlantic cod.

A pinball arcade for modern times

It started with falling in love with pinball, collecting machines and putting them out on route around Thunder Bay. When the Coo House came available, we turned it into the pinball pub we'd always wanted to share with the city. Whether you're new to the game or a pinball wizard from days past, you're welcome here.
