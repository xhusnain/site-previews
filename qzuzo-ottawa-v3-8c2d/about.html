About us

An Ottawa cleaning team built on care.

QZuzo started in 2026 with one idea: cleaning should be dependable, honestly priced, and done to a standard you notice the moment you walk in.

Our story

We are a locally owned team serving homes and businesses across Ottawa. We started QZuzo because too many people told us the same thing: cleaners who arrive late, skip corners and change every visit. We built the opposite, a small, careful team with one written standard for every space.

How we work

Small team. High standards.

Every clean follows the same checklist, whether it is a weekly home visit, an office after hours or the last clean before a move. We would rather do fewer jobs properly than rush through more.
