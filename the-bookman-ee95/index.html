  • Appraisals

About The Bookman

The Bookman has been a new, used and rare bookstore in some form since the early 1970s. It began in a three-room house on Euston Street as P.E.I.'s first used book store, moved to Kent Street in 1990 when the current owner bought it, and has been in the Currie building on Queen Street since 1995. The operative words are quality and affordable.
