Dental care for the whole family

  • Exams and preventive care

    Thorough exams of teeth and gums, oral cancer screening, x-rays when needed, cleanings, sealants and fluoride.

  • Restorative dentistry

    Comprehensive treatment planning to restore your teeth using conservative procedures.

  • Cosmetic dentistry

    Treatments that bring out the natural look of your smile and last.

  • Dental emergencies

    If an emergency happens, we make every effort to see you as soon as possible.

Dental health care, not disease care

We believe prevention and education are the keys to good dental health. That's why every visit starts with a careful look at your teeth, gums and medical history. Infection control matters to us: we follow the sterilization standards recommended by the ADA, OSHA and CDC to protect our patients and our team.
