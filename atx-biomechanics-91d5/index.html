How it works

Three steps. One body.

Every 1:1 session focuses on improving your posture and gait, the foundations everything else is built on.

01 /

Assess

We look closely at how you stand, walk, run and throw to see the patterns your body has settled into.

02 /

Retrain

Guided 1:1 work to retrain the movement patterns behind chronic pain and lost performance.

03 /

Move better

Carry the new patterns into daily life and sport. Move with less pain and more efficiency.
