Family owned for over 20 years

Linguine's Italian Restaurant has served Belleville and the Quinte area for over 20 years. We're family owned and operated, have won Readers' Choice Awards for favourite Italian restaurant, and have sponsored the City of Belleville and many civic organizations. We're within walking distance of several hotels.
