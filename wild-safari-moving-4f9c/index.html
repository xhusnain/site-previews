Questions

Before you book

How far ahead should I book?

Two to four weeks is ideal, and earlier for end-of-month and summer weekends. Short-notice moves are often possible, so just ask.

Do you take beds and furniture apart?

Yes. Disassembly and reassembly are part of every move.

Can you pack for me?

Yes, full or partial packing, and we can bring boxes and materials ahead of time.

Do you move out of state?

Yes. Long-distance moves get one point of contact from pickup to delivery.
