Welcome to Vista Hermosa

Vista hermosa means beautiful view. Our cedar-sided house sits on Main Street in Tofino, overlooking the harbour and Clayoquot Sound, where eagles fly past the living room and seals play in the water below. Chesterman Beach is a five-minute drive away. Guests come back year after year, in stormy weather and sunshine alike. Your host is Eric Jensen.
