Our story

Run2Patty is a family-owned take-out and catering company founded by Chef Jacob Henry, a graduate of the Culinary Management program at Algonquin College. Coming from a Jamaican background, we specialize in Caribbean cuisine. We started with homemade Jamaican beef patties and have grown to jerk chicken, oxtail, curry goat, roti, rice and peas, fried fish, plantain and much more.
