Treatments

  • Cleanings and check-ups

    Regular cleanings and exams to keep your natural teeth for life and catch gum disease early, plus oral cancer screening.

  • White fillings

    Tooth-coloured restorations that blend in with your smile.

  • Crowns and bridges

    Crowns to restore teeth with too much missing structure or after a root canal, and bridges to replace one or a few missing teeth.

  • Root canal therapy

    Treatment for teeth with infection, large decay or a fracture that reaches the nerve.

  • Wisdom teeth removal

    Wisdom teeth extractions performed in-office by a dentist with a special interest in oral surgery.

  • Laughing gas, whitening and guards

    Nitrous oxide to ease dental anxiety for adults and children, professional teeth whitening, TMJ therapy, and sports and night guards.

Meet Dr. Rohit James

Dr. Rohit James, B.D.S., D.M.D., earned his dentistry degree from Christian Dental College, Ludhiana, in 2009 and completed the advanced standing program at Boston University in 2012. Before moving to Chatham, he practised for four years as an associate dentist in Petawawa, Ontario, and has developed a special interest in oral surgery, including wisdom teeth extractions. He is a member of the Canadian Dental Association, the Royal College of Dental Surgeons of Ontario, the Ontario Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry. Returning patients are always welcome: we keep your records, so coming back is easy.
