What we offer

  • Original paintings

    Original oil and acrylic paintings from local and international artists, plus custom painting.

  • Art classes

    Adult and kids classes Monday to Sunday: acrylic, watercolour, sketching and shading, cartooning and crafts.

  • Family Sunday workshops

    Two hours of drawing and crafts, and you take your project home. Birthday parties too.

  • Rent or lease art

    Art rental for businesses and art lovers, with weekly terms and lease to own.

  • Custom frames and canvas prints

    Wood frames for your paintings and canvas prints of your photos. Contact us for a quote.

A gallery by the lakefront

2 For Art is a gallery by the Mississauga lakefront with pieces ranging from classical to contemporary. We work with artists of many styles and aim to make original art a captivating but affordable experience.
