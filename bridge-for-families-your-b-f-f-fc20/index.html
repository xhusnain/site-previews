What I can offer your family

  • Family counselling

    An initial meeting with everyone, individual sessions with each family member, then a family plan with practical strategies everyone agrees to.

  • Parents and youth

    Helping parents understand their children and teens, with support for ADHD, anxiety, autism, attachment, school issues and more.

  • Adult children and aging parents

    Helping adult children and their parents understand each other, including care-giving for aging parents.

  • Individual counselling

    Counselling for individuals and training for parents, covering relationships, separation, transitions and spirituality.

  • Programs

    Anger management, dealing with depression and anxiety, communication skills, emotional intelligence, learning strategies and safety plans for females.

About Lee-Anne

Lee-Anne is a Registered Social Worker with the Ontario College of Social Workers and Social Service Workers, a trained psychotherapist and a Certified Child and Youth Counsellor. She holds a Masters in Counselling, Psychotherapy and Spirituality from Saint Paul University and has over 23 years of experience with children, youth and parents in education, social service and group home settings. Sessions are $150 per hour, with prorated fees available and receipts for extended health insurance.
