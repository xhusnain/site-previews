About the Lion Motel

The Lion Motel is a family-run business that has welcomed dog show enthusiasts, bird watchers, boaters, campers, golfers, fishermen and business travellers for over twenty-five years. Cornwall, the US border and Upper Canada Village are ten minutes away, and Ottawa and Montreal are about 1.5 hours away. Book direct with us and pay no third-party fees.
