Sites and rentals

  • RV and tent sites

    Tent sites with water and electric, 30 amp back-ins, and 30 & 50 amp large pull-thrus. Full hook-ups available. Big rigs welcome.

  • Log cabins

    Log cabin sleeps 2 adults & 2 children; large log cabin sleeps 3 adults & 2 children. Now dog friendly, up to one dog per cabin. Bring your own linens.

  • Park trailer rental

    A park model trailer for rent by the night. No pets in the trailer.

  • Seasonal and bi-weekly sites

    Seasonal sites May 1 to October 31 with on-site winter storage included. Bi-weekly sites with winter water, satellite TV and metered electric. RVs 2015 or newer.

  • Amenities

    Indoor pool, newly renovated outdoor pool, indoor sauna, game room, cabana rentals, the Silver Valley Honky Tonk Barn, catch and release fishing ponds and kids train rides.

  • Activities

    A full 2026 schedule from Opening Weekend on, with ice cream socials, themed weekends and Friday night grilling on select nights.

About Silver Valley

Silver Valley Campsites is family owned and operated by John & Minnie Reid, open all year in the scenic Pocono Mountains. We're about 12 miles from Pocono Raceway, Camelbeach and the Pocono Premium Outlets, 13 miles from Mount Airy Casino, and close to Kalahari, Great Wolf Lodge, local wineries and five major ski slopes.
