The admin that runs itself

The repeat jobs behind every class, done automatically, so your team's time goes on teaching, events and growing Parrot Performers.

Trial to signed-up

Every trial family gets a friendly follow-up with the term price and a sign-up link. No one falls through the cracks on a busy Sunday.

Hi Sarah! Hope Ella loved her first class today. Spaces in Saturday Musical Theatre (6–9) are filling up; tap here to secure her place for the term.

Waiting lists

When a child leaves a full class, the space is offered to the next family on the list by text, with 24 hours to accept.

Good news: a space has opened in Junior Dance at Renfrew (Tuesdays 5pm). Reply YES within 24 hours to take it.

Term fees and reminders

Term invoices go out on their own, with a gentle reminder if unpaid, plus show dates and costume notes sent to the right classes.

Reminder: spring term fees for Ava's classes are due Friday. Pay here in one tap.
