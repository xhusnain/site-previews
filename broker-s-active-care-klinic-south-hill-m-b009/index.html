About the clinic

Dr. Blaine Broker graduated from the Canadian Memorial Chiropractic College in 1988 and has served Prince Albert ever since, moving into the current location on 2nd Avenue West in 2005. The clinic's chiropractors, including Dr. Scott Kurytnik and Dr. Zach Boettcher, work with all types of injuries and patients of any age, and believe in educating patients so they can stay flexible and pain-free long after treatment ends.
