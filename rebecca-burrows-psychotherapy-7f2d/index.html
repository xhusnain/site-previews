About Rebecca

Rebecca Burrows is a Registered Psychotherapist with the College of Registered Psychotherapists of Ontario (#007640). She holds a B.A. in Psychology and an M.A. in Counselling Psychology, and has specialized experience with sexual violence and complex trauma. Her approach is practical, solution-focused and client-centred, and clients take an active part in setting their goals.
