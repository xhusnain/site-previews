Therapy services

    Counseling to work through fear, stress, sadness and hopelessness and get back motivation and perspective.

About DeShannon

DeShannon Williamson, LCSW #99175, founded Comfort Wellness Therapy and wrote The MEG: The Mental Health Empowerment Group Workbook. He has worked with high-profile clients and professional athletes, and with the Probation Department, Kaiser Permanente, Alameda County Drug Court, Larkin Street Youth Services and Bay Area Community Services. He holds a Master's in Social Work and a Bachelor's in Human Development from California State University, East Bay, and chairs the Mental Health Committee for the Oakland Chapter of Omega Psi Phi and 100 Black Men of the Bay Area. His approach is trauma-informed, culturally grounded, warm and direct.
